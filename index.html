THE WHOLE GAME IN 20 SECONDS

How to play

  1. 01
    Peek at your role.

    The crew sees one secret word. The imposter sees no word at all.

  2. 02
    Give one clue.

    Take turns giving a short clue that fits the word without saying it outright.

  3. 03
    Talk it out, then vote.

    Discuss face to face. Everyone privately picks one person on their phone.

  4. 04
    Could the imposter still win?

    If the group votes wrong, the imposter gets one final guess at the secret word.

6–12 players · One imposter · Around 8 minutes
